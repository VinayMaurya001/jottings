Cert	Worth It for You?	Why
  LPI Linux Essentials	
    ❌ No	Too basic for your seniority
  CompTIA Linux+	
    ✅ Yes (optional)	Broad and beginner-friendly, decent value
  LFCS	
    ✅ Yes	Hands-on, cloud-aligned
  Real Projects	
    💯 Definitely	Demonstrates real experience in interviews


Start with LPIC-1 or go directly to LFCS (if you're confident):
  LPIC-1 – Learn real-world Linux admin (user mgmt, networking, syslog, bash scripting)
  LFCS (Linux Foundation Certified Sysadmin) – More hands-on and modern than LPIC-1
  Then LPIC-2 – For advanced concepts, networking, and scripting if you're moving toward DevOps/SRE
