Difference from other types
  Standard JEP → Fully supported, stable, production-ready.
  Preview JEP → Fully specified language/API feature, but released in “trial mode” to collect developer feedback before standardization. Enabled with --enable-preview.
  Incubator JEP → Introduces a new API in a special incubator module (jdk.incubator.*). It’s experimental but more structured than VM options.
  Experimental JEP → Mostly VM/GC/JFR/low-level features. No guarantee of future support; may be highly unstable.
                      Must be turned on using special JVM flags (e.g., --XX:+UnlockExperimentalVMOptions).
                      Is meant only for testing, research, and feedback, not for production use.
