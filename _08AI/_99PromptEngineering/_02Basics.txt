
How AI (LLM) Works – Fundamental Concept
	AI does NOT:
		Think
		Understand
		Reason like humans
	AI works on:
		Next-word prediction (probability-based model)



Important implication:
	AI generates answers based on:
		Training data patterns
		Prompt input
	So:
		Output = Function(Prompt + Patterns)


Why Same Question Gives Different Answers
	Reasons:
		Slight change in wording → different interpretation
		Context affects prediction
		AI is non-deterministic (not fixed output)


Hallucination
	AI gives:
		Incorrect
		Fabricated	
		Overconfident answers
	Why it happens:
		AI predicts → does NOT verify facts

	How to reduce hallucination:
		Give precise prompts
		Add constraints
		Ask for:
			Sources
			“If unsure, say I don’t know”



Iterative Prompting
	Ask initial prompt
	Check output
	Refine prompt
	Add constraints
	Repeat
