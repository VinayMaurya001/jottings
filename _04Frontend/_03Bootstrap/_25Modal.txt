The tabindex attribute controls an element’s focus behavior:
  tabindex="0"
    Puts the element into the natural tab order (in document order), so you can focus it by pressing Tab.
  tabindex="-1"
    Removes the element from the Tab sequence (you won’t land on it by pressing Tab), but it can still be focused programmatically (e.g. via JavaScript’s .focus()).
