Scrollspy
  To hook up Scrollspy in Bootstrap 5 so your navbar highlights the active section as you scroll, 
    you need two pieces:
      1)Markup on the scrolling container (often <body> or a specific <div>) with
        data-bs-spy="scroll"
        data-bs-target pointing to your navbar’s selector
        data-bs-offset to control when an item becomes active
        tabindex="0" to make it focusable 
      2)A nav with links whose href (or data-bs-target) matches the IDs of your page sections.
      3)Recommendation
          body{
              position:relative;
          }
