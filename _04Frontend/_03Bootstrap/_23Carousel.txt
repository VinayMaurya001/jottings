Carousel / Image Slider
  .carousel & .slide on the outer wrapper
    data-bs-ride="carousel" to start cycling automatically
  .carousel-indicators with <button>s to jump to slides
    data-bs-target="#myCarousel" data-bs-slide-to="2"
  .carousel-inner 
    .carousel-item
    .carousel-caption
  .active marks the starting slide
  Controls: .carousel-control-prev / .carousel-control-next
  .carousel-dark
  .carousel-fade
    for a fade transition
  data-bs-interval="2000"



Responsive Considerations
  Images: use .img-fluid or responsive srcset for varying screen sizes.
  Captions: wrapped in .carousel-caption; hide on small screens with .d-none d-md-block.
  Controls/Indicators: always keyboard-accessible and screen-reader friendly.
