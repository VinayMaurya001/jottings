Progress Bar

  Basic Progress Bar
    .progress
    .progress-bar
    style="width: 50%;" 
    <div class="progress">
      <div class="progress-bar" role="progressbar" style="width: 50%;" aria-valuenow="50"
          aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100">
        50%
      </div>
    </div>
