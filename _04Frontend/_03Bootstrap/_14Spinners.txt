Spinners/Loaders
  In Bootstrap 5, spinners are used to show loading states in your UI. 
  1. Border Spinner
    .spinner-border
    .spinner-border-sm
    <div class="spinner-border text-primary" role="status">
      <span class="visually-hidden">Loading...</span>
    </div>
  2. Grow Spinner
    This one "grows" instead of spinning with a border.
    <div class="spinner-grow text-success" role="status">
      <span class="visually-hidden">Loading...</span>
    </div>
    .spinner-grow
    .spinner-grow-sm
  Inside Buttons
    You can show a spinner inside a button to indicate an ongoing action:
    <button class="btn btn-primary" type="button" disabled>
      <span class="spinner-border spinner-border-sm" role="status" aria-hidden="true"></span>
      Loading...
    </button>
