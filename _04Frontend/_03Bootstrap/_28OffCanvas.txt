OffCanvas
  OffCanvas  is similar to modals, except that is often used as a sidebar navigation menu.
  Bootstrap 5’s Offcanvas component lets you create hidden side panels (or top/bottom drawers) that slide into view. 
    It’s great for navigation, filters, or any sidebar content. 
  Basic Markup & Trigger
    .offcanvas 
      .offcanvas-start slides in from the left.
      .offcanvas-header
      .offcanvas-title
      .offcanvas-body
    The trigger uses data-bs-toggle="offcanvas" and 
      data-bs-target="#offcanvasExample" on <button> or
        href="#offcanvasExample" on  <a> tag.
    Offcanvas must have tabindex="-1" so it can be focused for accessibility.
  Positions
    .offcanvas-start
    .offcanvas-end
    .offcanvas-top
    .offcanvas-bottom
  Options & Attributes
    data-bs-scroll="true" → allow body scrolling while offcanvas is open.
    data-bs-backdrop="false" → disable the backdrop overlay.
