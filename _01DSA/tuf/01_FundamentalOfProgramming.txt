What Is a Program?
	A program is a set of instructions that tells the computer what to do.


What Is a Bug?
	A bug is a error or mistake in the program.
	Fun fact: In 1947, a computer stopped working. Engineers searched and found a real insect (a moth) stuck inside the machine! They removed the "bug" and the computer worked again. The name stuck.

Debugging
	Finding & fixing bugs
	Debugging is the process of finding and fixing bugs.
	As programmers, we spend a lot of time debugging. It is normal. Even the best programmers create bugs. 
		The skill lies in finding and fixing them.
