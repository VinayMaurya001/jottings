
Parts of speech
  Parts of speech are words that have a standalone function.
    The function could be to describe actions,
      or to join clauses,to describe objects,to show their attributes like size, shape, taste or location.

  Noun
    What
    Who 
  Verb
    What to do?
  Adjective
    Attribute of a noun or describe the noun
  Adverb
    Modifies a verb, an adjective or another adverb
    How ?
  Pronoun
    To replace a noun
    I, you, he, she, it, we, they, 
    what, this, that, these, those
    him
  Preposition
    A preposition is a word placed before a noun or pronoun to show its relationship with another word in the sentence.
    in, on, of, at, to, under, over, before, after
    at, in, on, by, with, for, from, to, of, about, over, under, after, before, between, during, into, onto, towards, near, beside, above, below, across, through, around, against
  Conjunction
    A conjunction is a word that joins words, phrases, or clauses in a sentence.    
    and but


Types of Pronouns (with list)
  1. Personal Pronouns
    Subjective: I, you, he, she, it, we, they
    Objective: me, you, him, her, it, us, them
  2. Possessive Pronouns
    mine, yours, his, hers, its, ours, theirs
  3. Reflexive Pronouns
    myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves
  4. Demonstrative Pronouns
    this, that, these, those
  5. Interrogative Pronouns
    who, whom, whose, which, what
  6. Relative Pronouns
    who, whom, whose, which, that
  7. Indefinite Pronouns
    anyone, anybody, everyone, everybody, someone, somebody, no one, nobody,
    each, either, neither, all, some, many, few, several, none, one, both, much, everything, anything, nothing
  8. Reciprocal Pronouns
    each other, one another
  9. Distributive Pronouns
    each, either, neither
