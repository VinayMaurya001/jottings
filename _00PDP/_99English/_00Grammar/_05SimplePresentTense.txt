Simple Present tense
  How to use it 
      action events in the present time 
      routines or regular events
        but not happening at the moment
      
      I go shopping for food three times a week.
      She drinks.
      I drink.
      THey drink.

      subject + base form of a verb(+s)

      negative  
        do/does not  + verb(-s)
        My mom does not like meat.

      question  
        (Do/does + subject) + verb(-s)
        When do you work?
        Do you work?
        What do you do?

      Auxiliary word
        Grammer word
        Without any meaning
        Helps you to understand the tense of the question

      Who works with ten other people.
        No Auxiliary verb here due to unknown question word Who.
      Who does Tom work with?
      What happens to water in the freezing temperature?
        what - unknown subject 
        happens- predicate
        predicate always comes after subject.
  
  When to use it
    1. to describe permanent situations or facts
        Americans drinks a lot of coffee.
        A beautiful white tiger lives in our zoo.
        I live in INDIA.
    2.routines/regular basis
        I go shopping for food three times a week.
    3. Schedules
      We'd (we had) better hurry.The plane leaves in 30 minutes.
    4. to narrate a story
      ok, there is this old man in thick glass & funny hair.
        I go to him and ask....
    5. In newspaper headlines to show past event
        Chinese chipmaker releases a new, more powerfull processor 
    6.formal speech
        when writing a mail
        I look forward to hearing from you soon.
