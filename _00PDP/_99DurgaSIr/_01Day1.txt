1.Stop Doing Everything Yourself

Ravi
  First Online video course launch
    1.Writing the content
   2.Recording the videos
   3.Editing the videos
   4.Thumbnail design
   5.Creating the course website
   6.Planning payments & marketing

  Ravi's wrong mindeset
    1.If I want top quality, then myself have to do.
    2.I cannot afford to hire someone (money problem)
    3.Other may not understand my style or standard
  
  New thinking
    1.Trying to do evrthing alone is the reason I am slow
    2.Even if I get 1 helper, I will move faster
    3.I dont need to do everthing. I need to build a support team

  Result
    1.He hired a video editor part time.
    2.He hired a Virtual assistent

  30 days only his course launched
  Sales started
  pay his team and reinvested
  free & focused

  Lesson
    Doing everthing yourself feels safe. But it is actually slow & tiring.
    Except yor core work for other works
      Stop asking- How can I do this?
      Start asking- Who can do this for me?  
    Completing with 80% perfection is better than leaving goal completely
